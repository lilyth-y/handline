    Privacy notice

    📷 Your palm photo

    Your photo is analyzed only inside your phone (browser). It is never uploaded or stored.

    📊 What we collect anonymously

    When the analysis finishes, we send numbers only. They are used solely to recalibrate Hoa's “top %” reference against real phone photos.

    • Palm-line measurements (length, curve, clarity, start/end position — as ratios of palm width)
    • Your result type, the 4 star ratings and the number of lines found
    • Photo width and height, app and model versions, device category (in-app browser / mobile / desktop)
    • The date sent (the time of day is not recorded)

    👀 Visit statistics

    We count visits anonymously with Vercel Web Analytics. No cookies are used; we only gather page views, referrers, browser and device type, and a country-level region estimated from the IP address. The IP address itself is not stored.

    📣 Ads

    Google AdSense ads may appear below the result screen. Ads are served by the provider, which may use cookies or advertising identifiers under its own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Ads pages for details.

    🚫 What we don't collect

    We don't store photos, faces, names, contact details, accounts, device IDs, IP addresses, exact location or exact time. The stored numbers alone can't tell anyone whose hand it was. (A short-lived server access log made when the numbers are sent is kept briefly in Supabase for security and then deleted automatically.)

    🔒 Storage and use

    The numbers are stored in a Supabase database (Seoul region). The app can only send them, not read them. They are not used for anything other than calibration and are not sold to third parties.

    ⚙️ Turn off

    When off, this device won't send palm measurements. The setting is remembered in this browser. Visit statistics are counted anonymously, separately from this.
