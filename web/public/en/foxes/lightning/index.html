What kind of fox is this?

The Lightning-Hand Idea Fox has a hand that flashes onto a different path even in an ordinary scene. When a line has many branches and the head line curves softly, old tales read wit and quick reflexes. In Hoa the Fox Sage's eyes, this type changes the air with a single idea, whether in a meeting room or an alley. Lightning is short and bright, so if you catch what pops up and act on even a little of it, your talent stays behind as light.

Shades of this aura
