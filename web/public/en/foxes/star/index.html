What kind of fox is this?

The Starlight-Hand Wisdom Fox has a hand that gazes quietly at the blurry and picks out only the core, clearly. In traditional palmistry, a long, straight head line was read as a sign of analysis and focus. In Hoa the Fox Sage's eyes, this fox finds its way by the stars even on waves of emotion. But over-analysis can slow decisions, so practicing trust in "enough information right now" suits you.

Shades of this aura

  • Analysis and focus that calmly splits things apart
  • Trusts verified facts over groundless talk
  • A knack for putting complicated things in order
  • Hungry for learning and digs in for a long time
  • When the head runs ahead, remember to keep the heart's temperature too
