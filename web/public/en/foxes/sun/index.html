Sun-Hand Leader Fox

#Leadership #Confidence · Sun energy

A sun-bright aura radiates from your hand. You have a leader's confidence — people just naturally follow.

What kind of fox is this?

The Sun-Hand Leader Fox has a hand that brightens the air the moment it walks into a room. In traditional palmistry, a heart line pointing toward the index finger or a long fate line spoke of a front-runner aura. In Hoa the Fox Sage's eyes, this fox does not run from responsibility and makes a place people can lean on. The brighter the light, the longer the shadow, so the room not to carry everything alone makes a better leader.

Shades of this aura

  • Confidence to stand in front and light the direction
  • Draws people's eyes naturally
  • The strength to share goals in words and lead them into action
  • Does not dodge responsibility when it is given
  • Knows that to light everyone, you need nights of your own too
