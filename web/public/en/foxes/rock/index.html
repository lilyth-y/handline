Rock-Hand Steadfast Fox

#Grit #Steadiness · Rock energy

Rock-solid grit is carved into your palm lines. Once you start, you finish — nobody can match your staying power.

What kind of fox is this?

The Rock-Hand Grit Fox has a hand that does not easily waver once it picks its spot. When the lines are bold and the life line runs long, old palm books spoke of endurance and trustworthiness. In Hoa the Fox Sage's eyes, this type does not give itself over to trends. It may seem slow, but it ends up waiting at the finish. Still, even a rock gets a path carved by water that flows long enough. Telling stubbornness from conviction makes you sturdier.

Shades of this aura

  • Persistence to take responsibility for what it starts, to the end
  • Builds trust through results rather than words
  • Does not sink in difficult stretches
  • Trusts accumulation over change
  • Refine stubbornness into conviction and others can lean on you
