📜 The 12 Foxes

Ho ho, which fox is hiding in your hand? Peek at all twelve, one by one. With just one palm photo, โฮอา จิ้งจอกเซียน will point right to yours.

โฮอา จิ้งจอกเซียน sets the grain of your palm against traditional palmistry rules and picks the closest of twelve fox types. The dex below gathers each fox's aura, temperament, and the pairings traditional lore calls a good match. The in-app friend compatibility reading is a separate, just-for-fun calculation; the pairings in this dex are old palmistry stories about aura.
