What kind of fox is this?

The Petal-Hand Popular Fox has a hand that brightens a room with a single word or glance. In traditional lore, a heart line with many twigs and a clear love-luck star was read as a lovable charm. In โฮอา จิ้งจอกเซียน's eyes, this fox draws people without trying to dress up. Popularity is also a wind, so with an eye to choose the connections you truly want to stay with, the flower blooms longer.

Shades of this aura

  • Sociable charm that makes friends quickly anywhere
  • Reads mood and timing by feel
  • Giving and receiving compliments and attention comes naturally
  • Quick to start relationships, leaving a lasting impression
  • Shines when choosing depth among many connections

Foxes that pair well

The stories below are old palmistry lore about aura: pairings said to suit each other because their grains match or because they fill in each other's gaps. They are not scores measured from palms, so please take them only as a light reference.

  • ☀️ จิ้งจอกผู้นำมือตะวัน — Leading Sun-Hand and beloved Petal-Hand are said to make each other shine in the middle of a gathering.
  • 🌌 จิ้งจอกโชคดีมือกาแล็กซี — When Petal-Hand's flowing charm gains Galaxy-Hand's harmonious luck, connections and chances were said to open evenly.
