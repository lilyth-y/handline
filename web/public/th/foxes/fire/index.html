What kind of fox is this?

The Flame-Hand Passion Fox has a hand that, once its heart is set, pushes on even through the night. In traditional palmistry, this kind of vitality was read from a wide life-line arc and a bold, thick line. In โฮอา จิ้งจอกเซียน's eyes, this fox is not afraid of the spark of a beginning. But fire handled well becomes warmth, and left alone can burn itself, so keep a rhythm for rest and it will burn longer.

Shades of this aura

  • Drive to run to the end once its mind is made up
  • Pours energy into gatherings and work without holding back
  • Frank in showing feelings, with a high temperature
  • Short hesitation before new challenges
  • When passion runs too hot, needs the wisdom to count rest as a skill too

Foxes that pair well

The stories below are old palmistry lore about aura: pairings said to suit each other because their grains match or because they fill in each other's gaps. They are not scores measured from palms, so please take them only as a light reference.

  • 🍃 จิ้งจอกอิสระมือลม — When the Flame's passion gains the Wind's adventure, the aura was said to spread farther. A combination that burns without getting blocked.
  • ☀️ จิ้งจอกผู้นำมือตะวัน — When blazing drive overlaps with bright leadership, they easily become a pair who run side by side at the front.
