🏆 Fate Line

If you see a vertical line rising from your wrist toward the middle finger, that is called the fate line. If you do not have one, nothing is strange at all, so do not be alarmed.

What kind of line is it?

The fate line starts near the wrist and runs vertically up the palm toward the base of the middle finger. Unlike the life, head and heart lines, it is not clearly present on every hand. Plenty of hands have it faint, only partway, or hardly visible at all.

In traditional palmistry the fate line is told as the line of your working path, how you step out into the world, and the flow where you meet your surroundings. The name "fate" makes it easy to think everything is already decided, but Hoa Cáo Tiên does not see it that way. This line is less a fixed script than a story about how a person pushes ahead on their own road.

In traditional palmistry

A line rising clearly from the wrist was said to mean a clear direction from early on, and one that only appears in the middle of the palm to mean your path sharpens in mid-life. A line branching up from the life line was read as work beginning from family influence or your close surroundings, and one rising straight from the center of the wrist as a clearly self-set goal.

A line stretching long toward the fingers meant strength to keep going toward a dream, and one that breaks or veers sideways many stories read as a career change or a shift of role. Many branches meant varied interests and activities, and meeting other lines was sometimes read as a person or event changing the flow of your work.

A hand with no visible fate line is by no means an "unlucky hand." Old tales often read such hands as the type who make their own road rather than follow a set one. Hoa Cáo Tiên likewise never forces a missing line into an ominous reading.

How the Hoa Cáo Tiên app looks at it

The app first checks whether a fate line is visible in the flat photo, and if so measures its relative length against palm width and looks for breaks and branches. It does not measure the 3D depth of your hand.

If the line is faint or overlaps creases, "present" and "absent" can flip depending on the photo. A photo where your wrist is not cut off and the whole palm is evenly lit helps the measurement. The fate line varies a lot from person to person, so not finding one is not a failure.

The app's career luck story is the result of setting this line's presence, length and relation to other lines against traditional rules. It is not a diagnosis for choosing a career, so please enjoy it only as a hint, just for fun.
