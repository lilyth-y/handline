Cáo hòa bình tay mây

#Hòa bình. · khí Mây

Một đám mây mềm mại, thanh bình bao quanh anh. Sự ân cần dịu dàng của anh, bao bọc mọi người một cách thoải mái, là kho báu của anh.

What kind of fox is this?

The Cloud-Hand Peace Fox has a hand that lowers the temperature even in a noisy room. When the head and life lines start together or the lines are clear and tidy, old palm books read caution and harmony. In Hoa Cáo Tiên's eyes, this type is less about winning fights than about wrapping things so a bond does not break. Since consideration is a habit, voicing your own discomfort with the same weight makes relationships last longer.

Shades of this aura

  • Consideration and patience that put those around at ease
  • Looks for a way rather than raising a voice in conflict
  • Steadiness that keeps its rhythm without hurrying
  • Notices small discomforts and tends to them first
  • Do not only watch others' hearts; let your own cloud rest too

Foxes that pair well

The stories below are old palmistry lore about aura: pairings said to suit each other because their grains match or because they fill in each other's gaps. They are not scores measured from palms, so please take them only as a light reference.

  • 🪨 Cáo kiên cường tay đá — When gentle consideration meets sturdy grit, one wraps around the heart and the other becomes the pillar, so it was said.
  • 🌊 Cáo đồng cảm tay sóng — Peaceful Cloud-Hand and gentle Ripple-Hand were seen as a warm pair who look after each other without hurrying.
