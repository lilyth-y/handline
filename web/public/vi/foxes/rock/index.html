Cáo kiên cường tay đá

#Tính cách của người khác · khí Đá

Độ bền đá được khắc vào đường lòng bàn tay. Một khi đã bắt đầu, bạn sẽ hoàn thành nhiệm vụ — không ai có thể sánh bằng quyền lực của bạn.

What kind of fox is this?

The Rock-Hand Grit Fox has a hand that does not easily waver once it picks its spot. When the lines are bold and the life line runs long, old palm books spoke of endurance and trustworthiness. In Hoa Cáo Tiên's eyes, this type does not give itself over to trends. It may seem slow, but it ends up waiting at the finish. Still, even a rock gets a path carved by water that flows long enough. Telling stubbornness from conviction makes you sturdier.

Shades of this aura

  • Persistence to take responsibility for what it starts, to the end
  • Builds trust through results rather than words
  • Does not sink in difficult stretches
  • Trusts accumulation over change
  • Refine stubbornness into conviction and others can lean on you

Foxes that pair well

The stories below are old palmistry lore about aura: pairings said to suit each other because their grains match or because they fill in each other's gaps. They are not scores measured from palms, so please take them only as a light reference.

  • ☁️ Cáo hòa bình tay mây — When gentle consideration meets sturdy grit, one wraps around the heart and the other becomes the pillar, so it was said.
  • 🪙 Cáo tài lộc tay vàng — Meticulous Golden-Hand and Rock-Hand, who sees things through once started: the story goes that plans and action lock together firmly.
