Meet Hoa Cáo Tiên

Ho ho, I am Hoa Cáo Tiên, the white nine-tailed fox. I look over your palm with my fox eyes and point out which of the twelve foxes you are closest to.

About the service

Xem chỉ tay Hoa Cáo Tiên is a web service that finds the life, head, heart and fate lines in a single photo of your palm and sets them beside traditional palmistry lore. Results come as one of 12 fox types plus star ratings for wealth, love, health and career luck. Your photo is analyzed only inside your phone (browser) and is never sent to a server or stored.

The readings are xem chỉ tay chỉ để giải trí. They are not a diagnosis or prophecy, and do not replace medical, legal, financial or career advice. The same hand can look a little different depending on the light and angle of the photo.

If you are curious about the basic lines, read the Cẩm nang chỉ tay; if you are curious about the twelve foxes, read the Tủ linh cáo first. To see your result right away, all it takes is one palm photo.
